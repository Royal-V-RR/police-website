Westgate Residents Report Disturbing Sightings

Several residents of the Westgate District have come forward this week to report what they describe as unexplained phenomena near the disused warehouse block on Copperfield Lane. The Procyon Police Department confirmed it is "aware of the reports" but declined to provide further comment.
